# Funsy

Funsy plays funscripts on your Handy, Bluetooth toys and strokers in sync with your videos, on your desktop or in VR.

- Website: https://funsy-app.github.io/
- Downloads: [latest release](https://github.com/funsy-app/funsy-app/releases/latest)

For adults (18+).

This repository holds the website. The downloads are in [funsy-app/funsy-app](https://github.com/funsy-app/funsy-app/releases).
